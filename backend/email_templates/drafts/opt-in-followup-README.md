# Opt-In Follow-Up Sequence, five touches (Batch 2B draft)

**Status:** DRAFT. Not wired. Not sending. No marketing-automation platform is connected.

**Trigger (design intent):** the buyer explicitly checked marketing consent on the recommendation router, Safety Check follow-up, first-pull checklist form, or 12-questions form. Consent is stored separately from any transactional record. Non-consenting requesters never enter this sequence.

**Approvals required before wiring:** Vince Lawrence.

**Guardrails (locked)**
- No fake urgency, countdowns, or invented scarcity.
- No fear-heavy penalty repetition.
- No guarantee language, no "immunity to fines" claim.
- No case-study reference while CASE_STUDY_PUBLIC is false.
- No invented client stories, quotations, statistics, discounts, consultations, bonuses, or new offers.
- Every touch includes a working unsubscribe link.
- Sequence ends at Touch 5. No indefinite drip.
- If the buyer purchases, requests a fixed quote, or replies at any point, the sequence stops.

**Files**
- Touch 1, Day 0, delivery of the requested resource, `opt-in-followup-day-0.md`
- Touch 2, Day 2, one relevant gap category, `opt-in-followup-day-2.md`
- Touch 3, Day 5, a truthful educational scenario, `opt-in-followup-day-5.md`
- Touch 4, Day 8, present the recommended kit or service, `opt-in-followup-day-8.md`
- Touch 5, Day 14, one calm final follow-up, `opt-in-followup-day-14.md`
