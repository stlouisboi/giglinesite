## Description (both stores)
Fig is a personal wisdom app for people who want to live what they read.

Each day Fig gives you a few short insights from Scripture, Stoic philosophy, Christian writers and modern practice. Every card has one clear idea, how it shows up in real life, one action to try today and a question to reflect on.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 500 insights across six shelves: Biblical Wisdom, Relationships, Stoicism, Habits, Stewardship and Spiritual Formation
• Seven-day guided plans for worry, anger, waiting, temptation, marriage, discouragement, pride and more
• Ask Fig: ask about a real situation and get a short, honest answer drawn from your library, with the sources shown
• The Decision Room: talk a hard decision through, or work step by step: clarify, examine, challenge, act, review
• Revisit: Fig brings back what you saved at growing intervals so it sticks
• A private journal for what you tried and what you learned
• Write your own insight cards from your own reading

CLEARLY SOURCED
Every card is labeled Scripture, Christian interpretation, Stoic philosophy or Modern practice. Scripture is quoted from the New Living Translation. Exact quotations are marked separately from summaries.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account needed. Your reading, journal and progress stay on your device.

FIG PRO
Unlimited Ask Fig and Decision Room answers, narrated audio, connected wisdom across sources, and journal export. Yearly ($39.99, 7-day free trial) or monthly ($5.99). Cancel anytime.

Fig offers wisdom for reflection, not professional advice. For medical, mental health, legal or financial decisions, talk with a qualified professional.
