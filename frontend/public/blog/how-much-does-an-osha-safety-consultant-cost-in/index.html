What changes the quote

  • Facility size. More floor area and work zones require more review time.
  • Employee count. Headcount can change the records, training, and interview scope.
  • Number of locations. Every site has its own conditions and supporting records.
  • Facility complexity. Powered equipment, chemical use, fleet activity, and multiple shifts add work.
  • Condition of the records. Current files are faster to review than missing or scattered files.
  • Project scope. Diagnostic work and corrective implementation are separate engagements.

Free state consultation and private consulting serve different needs

North Carolina's OSH On-Site Consultation Program provides free, confidential assistance and does not issue citations or penalties during the consultation. However, participating employers agree to correct identified hazards by established due dates and provide written confirmation of correction. A private consultant offers a different arrangement, including privately selected scope, timing, deliverables, and follow-through.

Official sources: NC OSH On-Site Consultation Program and NCDOL consultation FAQs.

I do not describe the state program as an inferior option. It is a public service with its own process. Private consulting is a separate choice for employers who want to select the scope, timing, deliverables, and follow-through.

GigLine Safety & Compliance is an independent private consulting company and is not affiliated with OSHA or the North Carolina Department of Labor.

How to compare two private consulting quotes

Put the scopes side by side. Check locations, work areas, employee count, records, deliverables, and follow-through. Do not compare totals until those items match.
