# Incident Escalation Response Script

## When to use this

Any time a client reports a workplace incident, injury, near miss, hospitalization, fatality, or regulatory contact.

**Employer statutory reporting duties, act on these first:**

- **Fatality:** report to OSHA within **8 hours** (1-800-321-OSHA or the local Area Office).
- **In-patient hospitalization, amputation, or loss of an eye:** report to OSHA within **24 hours**.

**GigLine does not calculate these deadlines on the employer's behalf and does not contact OSHA on the employer's behalf under the standard monthly service.** The employer is solely responsible for meeting statutory reporting obligations.

GigLine response starts within one business day of the client's message. If the initial contact is by voice, GigLine follows up in writing within that same business day.

This script does not replace judgment. It is a scaffold to make sure nothing gets missed on the first call.

## Opening statement

> "Thanks for letting me know. Before we talk through anything, I need to ask four short questions to make sure we handle this the right way."

## The four questions

### Question 1

*Is the injured person receiving appropriate medical care right now?*

- If **no**, stop the script and direct the client to obtain immediate medical care. Nothing else on the script matters until the injured person is receiving care.
- If **yes**, proceed.

### Question 2

*Was there a fatality, an in-patient hospitalization, an amputation, or the loss of an eye?*

- If **yes**, note that this is an OSHA reportable event under 29 CFR 1904.39. The client, not GigLine, is responsible for the report to OSHA (8 hours for fatality, 24 hours for the others). Advise the client to report and to consult legal counsel and their insurance carrier. This event exceeds the standard remote-support allowance and requires a separately scoped engagement or a specialist referral.
- If **no**, proceed.

### Question 3

*Has OSHA or another regulator contacted the client already?*

- If **yes**, inform the client in writing that on-site inspection representation is out of scope under the standard monthly service. Offer to help the client find qualified representation (legal counsel or an EHS specialist with inspection-representation experience). Continue to be a documentation and program-history resource within the remote allowance.
- If **no**, proceed.

### Question 4

*Is the scene preserved as-is, or has it been altered for continued operations?*

- If **altered**, note the alteration for the file. Not a stopping condition, but a fact that will matter to any downstream investigation.
- If **preserved**, note that as well and advise the client on what to document while memory is fresh.

## Next-step guidance the standard remote allowance covers

If Question 2 and Question 3 both answered **no**, GigLine may provide, within the 45-minute monthly remote allowance:

- Guidance on what to document while memory is fresh (witness names, statements, photos of the scene, condition of equipment or PPE).
- Advisory on whether the event is likely OSHA recordable under 29 CFR 1904, noting that the final determination is the employer's.
- Suggestions for immediate corrective action to prevent recurrence.
- Confirmation of when the incident will be reviewed formally at the next scheduled service window.

## What GigLine does not do

- Contact OSHA or any other regulator on the client's behalf under the standard monthly service.
- Investigate the root cause of the event.
- Attend the scene under the standard monthly service.
- Provide an off-hours or weekend on-site response.

Any of the above require a separately scoped engagement in writing before work begins.

## Written follow-up

Within the same business day, send the client a written summary of the call using the following template.

```
Subject: Incident on [DATE], next steps

Hi [CLIENT NAME],

Thanks for letting me know about the [event type] that occurred on [DATE] at [LOCATION]. Here is my written summary of what we discussed:

1. Care confirmed: [YES / describe]
2. OSHA reportability triggers: [YES / NO, based on Q2]
3. Regulator contact to date: [YES / NO, based on Q3]
4. Scene condition: [PRESERVED / ALTERED, based on Q4]

Within the standard monthly service, I can help you with [list next-step guidance items agreed on the call]. This work will draw from the 45-minute remote-support allowance for this service month.

The following items are outside the standard monthly service and require a separate scope if you decide you need them: [list any that apply, e.g., root-cause investigation, OSHA inspection representation]. Let me know if you want me to scope any of that.

Please keep me posted on developments. I will follow up at our next scheduled visit unless something in the interim needs earlier attention.

Vince Lawrence
GigLine Safety and Compliance
(336) 329-8899
```

## File the record

Log the call, the questions answered, and the written follow-up in the client's private folder under `Incidents/YYYY-MM-DD-[short-description].md`. Add a row to the corrective-action tracker if the event triggers a corrective action.
