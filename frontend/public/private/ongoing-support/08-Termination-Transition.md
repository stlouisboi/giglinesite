## 1. Purpose of this document

This document is the final written deliverable from GigLine at the end of the Ongoing Safety Support engagement. It exists so that:

- The client knows the current state of every corrective action still open on the last day of the engagement.
- The next person or firm responsible for the client's safety program has a factual starting point.
- GigLine leaves a clean record of what was and was not delivered.

This is not a report on the client's overall compliance. It is a hand-off record.
