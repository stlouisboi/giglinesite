# GigLine — 90-Day Growth Execution Plan

**Goal:** Turn the now-clean website into a steady stream of booked walkthroughs.
**Owner:** Vince
**Budget cap (recurring):** ~$120/mo software + ~$300/mo paid ads = **~$420/mo total**
**One-time spend:** ~$50 (BrightLocal citation push)

Each item is a single focused work session. Tick them off in order. No skipping ahead — sequencing matters.

---

## PHASE 1 — FOUNDATION (Days 1–30)
*Goal: Get the trust signals + local rankings live so paid traffic in Phase 3 has a place to land.*

### Week 1 — Google Business Profile lockdown

- [ ] **Day 1:** Log into [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com). Confirm category is **"Safety Consultant"** (not "Business consultant" — too generic).
- [ ] **Day 1:** Add **service area:** Kernersville + 12 cities you have landing pages for. Match the city list exactly.
- [ ] **Day 2:** Add 8–12 real photos: shop floor walkthroughs (blur faces), reports you've delivered (blur client name), Vince in PPE, the truck/equipment.
- [ ] **Day 2:** Add **services as line items** with prices: "Safety Walkthrough & Top 10 Fixes Report — $650", "Documentation Review — $550", "Incident Review — $900".
- [ ] **Day 3:** Write 3 "GBP Posts" (Google's mini-blog feature) — short, 100-word posts with one photo each:
  - "What I look for in a forklift walkthrough"
  - "OSHA's #1 citation for small manufacturers"
  - "What a Top 10 Fixes report looks like"
- [ ] **Day 3:** Add the FAQ section in GBP (uses your 18 site FAQs as source).
- [ ] **Day 4–7:** **Review push to existing contacts.** Personally ask 10 past clients/contacts/network connections by name. Goal: 5 reviews by end of week. Send them this link:
      `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID`

### Week 2 — Citation cleanup

- [ ] **Day 8:** Sign up [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/) — pick the **Citation Builder** one-time push, ~$50.
- [ ] **Day 8:** Run the push to: BBB, Yelp, Manta, Thomasnet, Yellow Pages, Foursquare, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Chamber of Commerce listings, MapQuest. Use **exact same NAP** as your website footer (this is critical).
- [ ] **Day 9:** Manually claim these higher-value ones BrightLocal won't always get:
  - [Greater Winston-Salem Chamber](https://www.greaterwinstonsalemchamber.org/)
  - [Greensboro Chamber](https://www.greensboro.org/)
  - [High Point Chamber](https://www.highpointchamber.org/)
  - [NC Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP)](https://www.ies.ncsu.edu/)
  - [Piedmont Triad Partnership](https://www.piedmonttriadnc.com/)
- [ ] **Day 10:** Submit to NC-specific industry directories:
  - NC Association of Self-Insurers
  - NC Department of Labor partner list
  - Local SHRM chapter directories (HR people hire safety consultants)
- [ ] **Day 11–14:** **Continue GBP review push** — aim for **10 total reviews by end of Week 2.**

### Week 3 — Review automation + first content piece

- [ ] **Day 15:** Sign up [NiceJob](https://nicejob.com/) — $75/mo. Connect to your `walkthrough_requests` collection or just manually add past clients.
- [ ] **Day 15:** Configure NiceJob to text/email past clients **2 days after report delivered** with a 1-click review link.
- [ ] **Day 16:** Add NiceJob "Convert" widget to homepage (shows live review feed — social proof above the fold).
- [ ] **Day 17–19:** **Write 1 cornerstone case study** — pick your best past walkthrough. 1,200–1,500 words. Real photos. Anonymized client name. Title: *"How a [Industry] Operation in [City] Avoided $XK in OSHA Exposure — A Walkthrough Case Study."* Publish at `/case-studies/[slug]`.
- [ ] **Day 20–21:** **Post the case study to LinkedIn.** Write a 200-word teaser. Tag 3 NC manufacturing/HR people you know.

### Week 4 — Foundation review

- [ ] **Day 22:** Pull GBP Insights. How many "Direction requests," "Calls from listing," "Website clicks" this month? Baseline number.
- [ ] **Day 23:** Pull GSC data. Impressions in last 7 days? Top 5 queries you're showing for? Top 5 pages by clicks?
- [ ] **Day 24:** Run the SEO health check: `python3 /app/backend/scripts/seo_health_check.py`
- [ ] **Day 25:** **Reach 15 GBP reviews.** Tier 1 done.
- [ ] **Day 26–28:** Read your top 3 ranking pages and add 1 new section to each based on the search queries you're showing up for (per GSC). This is the *only* place "more content" actually helps right now.

**Phase 1 success metric:** 15+ GBP reviews, 50+ citations live, 1 case study published, baseline GSC numbers captured.

## PHASE 2 — AUTHORITY BUILDING (Days 31–60)
*Goal: Establish Vince as a recognizable voice in NC manufacturing safety. Pure off-site work.*

### Week 5 — LinkedIn rhythm

- [ ] **Day 31:** Update LinkedIn profile. Headline: "Safety Consultant for Small NC Manufacturers · OSHA 30-Hour · 25 yrs · giglinecompliance.com"
- [ ] **Day 31:** Add **Featured section:** link to /faq, /case-studies/[slug], /heat-guide.
- [ ] **Day 32–35:** Post 1 LinkedIn observation per day for 4 days. 100–150 words each. Real shop-floor stories (no client names). Examples:
  - "Saw this on a walkthrough today. Three things wrong with it. [photo of blocked panel]"
  - "OSHA's penalty for X just went up. Here's what changed."
  - "A common myth about HazCom programs."
- [ ] **Day 36:** Comment thoughtfully on 5 LinkedIn posts in your network's feed (manufacturers, HR people, plant managers). Goal: be seen as a real human, not a faceless brand.

### Week 6 — Reddit + niche forums

- [ ] **Day 38:** Create a Reddit account with your real name (vincelawrence-gigline or similar). Verify the email.
- [ ] **Day 38–44:** Spend **30 min/day** in r/OSHA, r/EHS, r/smallbusiness, r/manufacturing. **Read first, post second.** Answer 2 questions thoughtfully — 200+ word answers, real expertise, only link to your site if it's *genuinely* the best answer (not always).
- [ ] **Day 45:** Try the same on:
  - [iSixSigma forum](https://www.isixsigma.com/forum/)
  - [SafetySmart Compliance Forum](https://safetysmart.com)
  - Quora — same approach as Reddit

### Week 7 — Pitch one trade publication

- [ ] **Day 46:** Pick **one** publication. Recommended targets:
  - [EHS Today](https://www.ehstoday.com/)
  - [Occupational Health & Safety](https://ohsonline.com/)
  - [Triad Business Journal](https://www.bizjournals.com/triad)
  - [Business North Carolina](https://businessnc.com/)
- [ ] **Day 46–47:** Read 5 of their recent articles. Note who the editor is.
- [ ] **Day 48:** Draft a pitch email — 150 words max. Suggest 3 article angles. Sample subject: *"Guest article pitch: What a 25-year safety vet sees walking 200+ NC manufacturing floors."*
- [ ] **Day 49:** Send the pitch. Don't write the article yet — wait for response.
- [ ] **Day 50–52:** If no response in 4 days, follow up once with shorter email. Then move to next pub on the list.

### Week 8 — Second case study + review check

- [ ] **Day 53–55:** Write **case study #2.** Different industry than #1.
- [ ] **Day 56:** **Reach 20 GBP reviews.**
- [ ] **Day 57:** Update GBP with 3 more posts and 4 new photos (Google rewards activity recency).
- [ ] **Day 58–60:** Audit. Check GSC. Is your impression count up? Which pages get the most impressions? Are you ranking for any "city + walkthrough" queries yet?

**Phase 2 success metric:** 20+ GBP reviews, 30+ LinkedIn posts/comments, 10+ Reddit answers, 1 trade pub pitch sent, 2 case studies live.

## PHASE 3 — CONVERSION (Days 61–90)
*Goal: Turn the now-credible site into a predictable lead pipeline. Paid traffic only after the foundation is set.*

### Week 9 — Google Local Service Ads (LSA)

- [ ] **Day 61:** Apply for [Google LSA](https://ads.google.com/local-services-ads/). For your category, you'll need:
  - Business license (NC certificate of authority)
  - General liability insurance certificate
  - 3+ Google reviews already live (you have these now from Phase 1)
  - Background check ($30, Google runs it)
- [ ] **Day 62–68:** Wait for LSA approval (typical: 2–14 days). Don't try anything else paid yet.

### Week 10 — Google Search Ads (while waiting for LSA)

- [ ] **Day 69:** Create a Google Ads account if you don't have one. Connect to your existing GA4.
- [ ] **Day 69:** Build one campaign:
  - **Campaign name:** GL-Search-NC-Safety-Walkthrough
  - **Daily budget:** $10 ($300/mo cap)
  - **Locations:** 60-mile radius of Kernersville
  - **Keywords (exact + phrase match only — no broad):**
    - `[osha safety inspection greensboro]`
    - `[safety consultant winston salem]`
    - `"safety walkthrough nc"`
    - `[hazcom compliance help]`
    - `"osha consultant near me"`
    - `[forklift inspection company nc]`
  - **Negative keywords:** -training, -course, -online, -jobs, -hiring, -salary, -free
  - **Landing pages:** Send each ad group to the relevant city page or /services
- [ ] **Day 70:** Add **conversion tracking** to Google Ads. Trigger = `/request-walkthrough` form submission (you already track this via UTM attribution → admin dashboard). Confirm it's firing.
- [ ] **Day 71–75:** Let it run. Check daily for the first week. Pause any keyword with $20+ spent and 0 conversions.
- [ ] **Day 75:** Add Google Ads attribution to your admin dashboard query — your existing UTM capture should already grab `gclid` and `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc`.

### Week 11 — LSA active + retargeting

- [ ] **Day 76:** Once LSA is approved, set budget to $300/mo (Google charges per qualified lead, not per click — ~$15–35 per lead in your category).
- [ ] **Day 77:** Confirm LSA shows the **Google Guaranteed badge** on your listing.
- [ ] **Day 78–79:** Set up **basic retargeting** in Google Ads. Anyone who visited but didn't submit /request-walkthrough sees a banner ad for the next 30 days. $5/day, $150/mo cap.
- [ ] **Day 80–82:** Watch the call/form volume. By day 82 you should be getting **2–4 inbound calls/week** from combined paid + organic + GBP.

### Week 12 — Audit and 90-day review

- [ ] **Day 83:** Pull all metrics:
  - GBP reviews count, calls, direction requests
  - GSC impressions, clicks, top queries
  - Google Ads spend, conversions, CPL
  - Walkthroughs booked and revenue closed
- [ ] **Day 84:** Calculate **CAC (cost per booked walkthrough)** across each channel:
  - Organic / SEO: spend / bookings
  - GBP: harder to attribute, use call tracking number if needed
  - Google Ads: spend / form submissions
  - LSA: spend / qualified leads
- [ ] **Day 85:** Cut the worst-performing channel. Double down on best.
- [ ] **Day 86–90:** Draft **Plan B (Days 91–180)** based on what worked. Stop guessing — let the data drive Q2 strategy.

**Phase 3 success metric:** $300–500 CAC, 6+ booked walkthroughs in Q1 from new traffic, clear winning channel identified.

## DO NOT DO LIST (anti-checklist)

- ❌ Don't publish AI-generated blog content. Google's anti-spam systems are tuned for it.
- ❌ Don't pay an SEO agency that promises "guaranteed page 1 rankings." Nobody can guarantee that.
- ❌ Don't buy backlinks. Penalty risk is real.
- ❌ Don't enable Google Ads broad match — wastes 60%+ of budget in your niche.
- ❌ Don't run Facebook ads to a service that doesn't have impulse-purchase psychology behind it. Plant managers don't book walkthroughs from Facebook scrolls.
- ❌ Don't change your URL structure or canonical now that pages are indexed.
- ❌ Don't add 7 more city pages "just in case" — finish what you have first.
