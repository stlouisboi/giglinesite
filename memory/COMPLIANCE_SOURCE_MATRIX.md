## 6. 12 Questions (`/12-questions`)

Each of the 12 questions now:
- Uses the phrase **"What strong evidence may look like"** (previously "What the paper looks like").
- Names the applicable CFR paragraph or explicitly labels the item as a GigLine readiness benchmark rather than a universal federal requirement.
- Does not imply a universal written safety and health program is federally required for every employer.
- Distinguishes required OSHA 300/301/300A entries from partially exempt employers.
- Separates severe-injury reporting timing (8 hr / 24 hr) from routine recordkeeping.
- Labels root-cause and corrective-action tracking as GigLine practices.
- Names Emergency Action Plan applicability (1910.38) rather than claiming universal need.
- References 1910.212(a) for machine guarding rather than implying a universally required "machine-specific written analysis".
