# GigLine Google Business Profile Checklist

*Tailored for Vince Lawrence / GigLine Safety & Compliance. Ordered by impact per hour of work.*

---

## 1. Category (highest impact — do first)

**Primary category:** Safety Consultant

**Secondary categories** (GBP allows up to 9):
- Occupational Health Service
- Business Management Consultant
- Training Provider *(if you ever offer any)*

**Why it matters:** Your primary category is the single biggest factor in whether you rank for "OSHA consultant Kernersville" vs. nothing. "Safety Consultant" is the exact-match category Google uses for this search intent.

## 5. Photos (schedule 1 hour to knock these out)

Upload these specifically — Google rewards businesses with 10+ photos:

- **Logo** (square, 250×250 minimum)
- **Cover photo** (landscape — shop floor or warehouse shot with you in it works best)
- **At least 5 of Vince on-site** (hard hat, safety glasses, clipboard, in an actual facility — stock imagery hurts you)
- **Report example** (redacted — shows the deliverable)
- **Your vehicle** (if branded) or company van on-site
- **Before/after shots** (blocked electrical panel → cleared) — if you have any from past walkthroughs (with client permission)

**What NOT to upload:** stock images of OSHA signs, generic safety equipment photos. Google's algorithm can detect stock imagery and deprioritizes profiles that use it.

---

## 6. Post Weekly (this is what beats competitors)

Most local safety consultants never post on GBP. Posting weekly puts you ahead of 90% of them.

**4-week rotation:**
- **Week 1:** Link to a recent Field Note blog post with a 1-sentence hook
- **Week 2:** A specific OSHA citation stat (from homepage copy: "A single citation averages $15,625")
- **Week 3:** Service reminder ("Now scheduling walkthroughs for [month]")
- **Week 4:** Photo from a recent walkthrough + one takeaway

Each post = 250 words max, 1 image, 1 CTA button ("Call now" or "Learn more"). Takes 10 minutes/week.

## 7. Q&A (seed it yourself)

Most businesses ignore Q&A. Google shows these answers in search results.

**Post these questions from your own account, then answer them:**

1. *"How long does a safety walkthrough take?"*
   → "Most walkthroughs are 1–3 hours depending on facility size."

2. *"Do you report findings to OSHA?"*
   → "No. GigLine is a private engagement. Nothing leaves your facility except the report I hand you."

3. *"What areas do you serve?"*
   → "Within 60 miles of Winston-Salem — Kernersville, Greensboro, High Point, Burlington, Salisbury, and surrounding Triad communities."

4. *"How much does a walkthrough cost?"*
   → "Starts at $650 for small sites. Pricing depends on facility size and type. See giglinecompliance.com/services."

5. *"How soon do I get the report?"*
   → "Within 24–48 hours after the walkthrough."

## 8. Review Requests (highest leverage — trust signal for cold traffic)

**Current:** 1 review. **Target:** 5 reviews in 60 days, then 1 new review/month.

**How:**
1. Send the client-quote email template to 3 past clients this week (template saved from earlier in the project).
2. After every walkthrough, send a text/email 48 hours after delivering the report (when the client is happiest) with your review link:
   **`https://share.google/iUzTnuRSCNdguZQww`**
3. **Respond to every review within 48 hours.** Even negative ones. Google tracks response rate.

**Response template for 5-star reviews:**
> Thanks [Name] — glad the report gave you a clear picture of where things stand. Let me know when it's time for the next walkthrough. — Vince

---

## The one thing NOT to do

**Do not run GBP Ads.** They charge per click and for a local service business your money goes further in Google Ads (broader targeting) or just by letting organic GBP rank.

Managed platforms (Surefire Local, Podium, BirdEye, etc.) will push these — skip them at your volume. Platform fees of $300–$800/month only make sense past 100+ engagements/year.
