# GigLine Backlink Outreach Playbook
**For: Vince Lawrence · GigLine Safety & Compliance · Kernersville, NC**
**Drafted: Feb 2026**

This document contains 8 ready-to-send outreach emails plus targeting notes. Send 2–3 per week. Personalize the first sentence of each one. Do not send them all in one batch. Track responses in a simple spreadsheet — date sent, contact, response, follow-up date.

**Why these targets?** Each one is a `.org`, `.gov`, or `.edu` adjacent organization with an existing presence that ranks for compliance-related searches. A link from any of them carries domain authority that a hundred links from generic directories would not.

## EXECUTION NOTES

**Cadence:** Send no more than 2–3 emails per week. Spaced outreach feels intentional; bulk outreach feels like spam.

**Personalize before sending.** Replace the opening sentence with something specific to that recipient — a recent post they made, a member spotlight you saw, an event they ran.

**Follow up once.** If you haven't heard back in 10 business days, send a one-line reply on the original thread: "Just floating this up in case it got buried. Happy to provide anything that would help with the decision."

**Track responses.** Use a simple Google Sheet — date sent, contact, response, follow-up date, outcome. After 6–8 weeks you'll see patterns in who replies and who doesn't.

**What "success" looks like:**
- 8 emails sent → typically 2–3 responses → 1–2 directory listings or speaking inquiries
- Each directory listing or partnership announcement on a `.org` or `.gov` site is a high-quality backlink
- Each guest column or speaking gig generates additional inbound links over the following 6–12 months as people cite the work

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't pay for "guaranteed backlinks" through SEO services — they're penalty risk
- Don't reciprocate links with unrelated sites
- Don't use cookie-cutter templates without personalization

The links you build this way compound. They're slow to build, hard to lose, and the only kind Google's algorithm respects.
